## 2. 메이커 첫 댓글 (First Comment) — 영어

```
Hey Product Hunt! 👋🪩🌍

I built EmojiWorld because I kept noticing the same thing: my mom, my
7-year-old nephew, and my friends abroad all use the same 20 emojis —
but they can't use the same social network.

So I removed the one thing that divides us online: words.

On EmojiWorld:

🌍 Everything is emoji-only. A post looks like 😴💭☕ — and everyone
   on Earth understands it instantly.

❤️➡️📸 Photos are a privilege, not a default. Every 5 reactions you
   earn = 1 photo ticket. No photo spam, no influencer arms race.
   When you see a photo here, the community earned it.

🛡️ Hate comments are physically impossible. Comments are emoji-only.
   You cannot type an insult. This is the network I'd hand to my kid
   AND my grandma.

🎈 Guest mode = start in 3 seconds. No email, no password.

Would love your feedback — especially: what emoji categories do you
want next? Character/3D emoji packs are on the roadmap (EmojiWorld Plus).

Say hi in emoji below 👇😄
```
