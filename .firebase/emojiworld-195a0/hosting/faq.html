Frequently Asked Questions

What is the main purpose of EmojiWorld?

The main purpose of EmojiWorld is to create a fun and simple communication space where people can express their feelings and daily lives using only emojis, without the need for text in the main feed.

Can I use text at all on this platform?

In the main feed, posts are emoji-only to maintain the core concept. However, we plan to introduce a comment feature that will allow text-based communication on posts, blending the best of both worlds!

How do I earn points or climb the rankings?

You can earn points by completing daily missions, receiving reactions from other users, and actively participating in the community. Your rank is determined by your cumulative points.

Is my data safe?

Yes, we take data security very seriously. All user data is securely managed through Firebase Authentication and Firestore, and we do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent.

Why am I seeing ads?

To maintain and operate the EmojiWorld service for free, we run a limited number of advertisements. We strive to place ads in a way that does not disrupt your user experience. Revenue from ads helps us improve the service and develop new features.
